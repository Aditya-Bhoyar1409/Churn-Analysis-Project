
# Customer Churn Analysis & Customer Intelligence
Customer churn analysis using SQL, Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib and Seaborn.
## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer churn to understand which customers are leaving, why they are leaving, and when churn risk becomes significant.

The analysis combines customer demographics, subscription information, and customer support data to identify churn patterns and provide actionable business insights.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objectives of this project are:

- Identify customers who have churned
- Analyze churn patterns across subscription plans
- Analyze churn by state
- Understand the relationship between customer support escalations and churn
- Calculate key business KPIs
- Identify revenue and customer lifetime value at risk
- Provide actionable recommendations for customer retention

## 📈 Key Results

- Churn Rate: 28.6%
- Retention Rate: 71.4%
- Average Customer Tenure: 1,452 days
- ARPU: ₹18.85
- Revenue at Risk: ₹73.94K
- Escalation Rate: 19.85%
- Average Complaints per Customer: 0.43
- Correlation between escalations and churn: 0.77

## 💡 Business Insights

- Basic-plan customers showed the highest churn rate.
- Karnataka showed a particularly high churn rate.
- September 2024 showed the highest monthly churn in the analyzed data.
- Monthly-contract customers had substantially higher churn than annual-contract customers.
- Customer support escalations showed a strong positive relationship with churn.

## 🔍 Recommendations

- Investigate the reasons behind high churn in Karnataka.
- Review pricing or product changes affecting Basic-plan customers.
- Investigate the September 2024 churn spike.
- Prioritize customers with medium and high churn risk.
- Review complaints and support escalations for high-risk customers.
- Develop targeted customer-retention actions.
